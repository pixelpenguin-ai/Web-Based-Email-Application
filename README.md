#### Description:
The Web-Based Email Application is a versatile and user-friendly email platform designed to simplify your communication needs. With this application, users can seamlessly send, receive, and manage emails from anywhere with an internet connection.

Key Features:
Compose and Send: Create new emails with ease, addressing them to recipients, adding subjects, and composing messages effortlessly.
Inbox and Sent: Keep your emails organized with dedicated Inbox and Sent folders, making it simple to find and review your conversations.
User Authentication: Secure user accounts with password hashing, ensuring data privacy and protection.
Reply and Forward: Easily reply to received emails or forward important messages to others.
User-Friendly Interface: A clean and intuitive interface makes navigation and email management a breeze.
Responsive Design: Access your email from desktops, tablets, or mobile devices, with a responsive design that adapts to any screen size.
Whether you're a professional managing business correspondence or an individual staying connected with friends and family, the Web-Based Email Application provides a reliable and efficient way to stay in touch.
